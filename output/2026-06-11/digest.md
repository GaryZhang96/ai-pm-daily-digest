### AI & I by Every
**The Takeaway**: Automation with AI does not lead to fewer jobs; instead, it changes the nature of work, increasing the demand for experts.
Dan Shipper discusses the paradox that despite high levels of automation, the demand for human work, especially expert work, is increasing. He argues that AI makes previous expert competence widely available at a low cost, leading to an abundance of 'okay' work that requires expert refinement. This results in a higher demand for experts to turn this work into something valuable.
